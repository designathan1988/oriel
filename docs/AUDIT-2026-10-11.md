# FORMA → professional constructor: audit and plan

## Context

FORMA (`labs/building/forma`, app `src/app/editor.ts`, core `src/f3/*`) is Roadcraft's 3D building
constructor. The user wants to turn it into a professional application for good: build any kind of
building with tools made for it, a redesigned interface and everything non-destructive. I audited it
by using the app as an end user (server 5792, current code, 1280×960 window, pictures examined one
by one) and by reading the code. User decisions: **(1)** a 100 % non-destructive core, including
vertex/edge/face editing, with combining and uncombining elements (if no ready approach exists,
create one); **(2)** order: defects first, then interface and navigation, then the rest.

The audit was written in Portuguese while the interface was still in Portuguese; on 2026-10-11 the
whole application (code, interface, files, folders and docs) moved to English, so the interface
texts quoted below are given in English.

## 1. Audit (what I saw and the cause)

### A. Serious defects
1. **The sample project does not reopen.** `samples.ts:58` uses the color `#777`; the validator
   (`io/migrate.ts:382`, `/^#[0-9a-f]{6}$/`) refuses it and the app discards the saved project and
   falls back to the samples on every reload ("The saved project did not open… invalid color"). One
   invalid field rejects the whole project.
2. **A selection box that does not wrap.** The building's size handles and height dimension use
   `max(base+height)` of **all** solids, cutters included: on the "Courtyard block" (12.4 m) the
   handles ended up at 29 m, off screen, and the dimension said "↕ 29 m" (`editor.ts:433-436, 983`).
   The volume box is the wall prism: it stops at the eave and the roof sits above it (mansard,
   picture). Overhangs, balconies and parts (width) are not considered.
3. **Lines through the walls.** Outlines drawn with `depthTest:false` (`editor.ts:353`): the plan
   on the ground shows across facades and roofs like a "box" in the middle of the building.
4. **The yellow gizmo square does not move.** Dragging it changed the number of floors (4→3): the
   floors arrow, the X/Z arrows and the square share the same center; the hit test is by distance
   to the point (`handles.ts:163-191`), not by the drawn shape.
5. **"Use" does nothing.** It only stores `b.use` (`inspector.ts:348`); no effect on the model.
6. **Dimensions over the panels.** "↕ 12.4 m" and "↕ 7 m" fell on top of the inspector and the
   layers panel, covering the offset "Apply" button. "Frame" (F) ignores the islands: the building
   ends up behind the strip and the inspector (`view.ts:165`).
7. **Typed values fail.** After clicking a strip action (Offset), typing "-3"+Enter did not apply;
   clicking the measurements box lost the selection. The box is far from the object, at the bottom.
8. A hint that sticks after clicking ("Shortcuts · ?"); cut-off status bar text; a toast almost
   invisible over the light view; "Saving…" on selection.

### B. Interface
- The fixed "Modify" strip at the top takes 7–13 % of the height even when empty ("Select a
  building…"); with a volume it becomes two rows; the breadcrumb and the "View" island are pushed
  and the View island covers the model.
- Duplicated functions with different names: "+ Floor/− Floor" (strip and menu), "+ Storey/−
  Storey" (building panel and the volume's "Quick modeling"), the floors handle; Offset/Bevel/Split
  on the strip and again as forms in the inspector; material in three places (drawer, bucket,
  volume selects).
- Tools away from the object: move/rotate/scale/floors spread between the left bar, the strip, the
  inspector and the handles; nothing opens around the selection.
- The inspector is a long scrolling form; tool parameters mixed with properties; it redraws the
  whole `innerHTML` on every change (loses focus and clicks).
- Left bar: 13 unlabeled icons; "Add/subtract" is a hidden mode button.
- Size handles are 3D cubes (they show as large hexagons) — requested: smaller, standard.

### C. Navigation
Right button orbits, middle pans, the wheel zooms toward the **ground** under the cursor (not the
object), minimum distance 2 m, orbit around a fixed target, no orthographic views or view cube
(`editor.ts:1546-1603`, `view.ts:151`). Requested: middle drag = pan, wheel = zoom, Shift+middle =
orbit, zoom much closer.

### D. Selection
Only building → volume → side/top face; vertices only as plan handles; **horizontal edges,
polygons and roof faces are not selectable**; no selection modes, no saved selection sets, no
"select similar", no hide/isolate.

### E. Modeling tools
- Offset applies 1 m at once, without a preview or a handle; the value can only be changed in the
  bottom box. "As a new volume" creates a solid of the same height on top (coplanar faces,
  duplicated windows). An arc in the offset moved 0.96 m instead of 2 m (`modeling.ts:136-151`).
- Inference only in 2D on the plan (`infer.ts`): no snapping to 3D faces, no "on top/beside/
  inside/below".
- Booleans exist per volume (list order), but without groups or "uncombine"; no revolve, sweep,
  loft, radial/path array.

### F. Properties, layers, library, materials
- A building has only use, 4 floor fields (with "Rotation" inside Floors) and X/Z.
- Layers: three subjects in one list (layers, tree, categories), "Default ▾" repeated on every row,
  no type icons; eye/lock only on hover.
- Library: a 300 px drawer over the model; ~109 types in 49 families; blocks with a line icon;
  windows with 50 px thumbnails; no interior, vegetation or furniture; families only in code.
- Materials: 14 procedural finishes + 8 photographed, ~36 presets; material = finish + 2 colors + a
  few parameters, copied on each use (no project library or editor/composition).

### G. Measurements (5792, sample scene, 12 buildings)
193 draw calls, 85.7 k triangles, rebuilding a building 58–274 ms; handles recreate meshes on every
draw (`handles.ts:80`); `editor.ts` is 172 KB in one file.

## 2. Decisions (with the sources read)

1. **Non-destructive procedural document** (Blender modifiers, Fusion timeline, Houdini): each
   building is a graph of **nodes**: generators (parametric volume, free solid, revolved/swept
   profile), **operations** (boolean, offset, bevel, extrude face, inset, move vertex/edge/face,
   split, array, mirror) and **combination groups** (add/subtract/intersect as a node with live
   operands). "Uncombine" undoes the node and returns the operands intact; any node can be
   disabled, reordered or deleted and the result is rebuilt.
2. **Sub-element editing is an operation too**: selecting a vertex/edge/face stores a *persistent
   reference* (generator ID + side/vertex ID + Manifold face lineage —
   `originalID`/`reserveIDs`/`asOriginal`, manifoldcad.org/docs/jsapi) and the edit becomes a node
   ("move face 12 by 0.5 m"). If the reference disappears after an upstream edit, the node is
   marked orphan and reported (RESEARCH decision 12: never reposition silently).
3. **Revit/AutoCAD navigation** (help.autodesk.com, Revit "About Navigating Views"): middle = pan,
   wheel = zoom at the pointer, Shift+middle = orbit; pivot = selection or the point under the
   cursor; view cube. SketchUp/Blender presets optional.
4. **Gizmo like TransformControls r170** (source read): plane squares offset from the center
   (0.15), invisible hit meshes larger than the visible ones, ray against them, constant screen
   scale, near-edge-on handles hidden. Floors get their own handle, away from the center.
5. **Screen-space selection outline** (OutlinePass r170, read): visible edge and hidden edge
   (dashed/faint) by depth comparison; no lines without depth testing.
6. **Robust offset with Clipper2** (angusj.com, ClipperOffset/InflatePaths; port `clipper2-ts`):
   sharp/round/chamfer joins, collapsing parts vanish cleanly, arcs sampled and refitted.
7. **Parametric components with formulas** (SketchUp Dynamic Components: LenX, Copies, Copy,
   help.sketchup.com) on top of the existing family → type → occurrence (Revit).
8. **Selection modes** vertex/edge/face with 1/2/3 and Shift combining (Blender manual, read).

Each phase starts with the research required by CLAUDE.md (read sources, a message with links, then
code).

## 3. Phases

### Phase 0 — Seen defects (first)
- Colors: accept `#rgb`/`#rrggbb` and normalize; the validator **repairs with a note** instead of
  refusing the project; fix `samples.ts`; a round-trip test (save → open) of the samples.
- Real bounds: selection box, handles and dimensions from the evaluated geometry (`ev.shell` +
  parts, additive solids only), in the building frame; roof and overhangs included.
- Outlines with depth testing (visible strong, hidden faint) until the Phase 2 screen outline.
- Gizmo: hit test by the drawn meshes; the yellow square moves on the ground; floors away from the
  center.
- Dimensions and labels avoid the interface islands; `frame()` uses the free view rectangle.
- Measurements: typing after a strip action applies; clicking the box does not lose the selection.
- Inspector without a redraw that eats clicks/focus (update fields, not `innerHTML`).
- "Use" with an effect (full in Phase 4; here: floor heights and a commercial/industrial ground
  floor).
- Stuck hint, readable toast, cut-off text.
Files: `f3/model/samples.ts`, `f3/io/migrate.ts`, `f3/editor/editor.ts`, `f3/editor/handles.ts`,
`f3/editor/view.ts`, `f3/ui/inspector.ts`, `f3/ui/shell.ts`.

### Phase 1 — New interface (complete redesign)
- **Single command registry** (`f3/ui/commands.ts`, an evolution of `ui/actions.ts`): each function
  exists once, with one name (e.g. "Floor +/−"), icon, shortcut, condition; the bar, menu, search,
  HUD and shortcuts read from it. No more duplicates.
- Layout: a thin top bar (project menu, name/saved, **workspaces**: Model, Components, Materials,
  Interior, Present; undo, Ctrl+K search, export); a grouped left toolbar with submenus and labels
  on hover; a **contextual HUD next to the selection** (move, rotate, scale, floor ±, enter,
  duplicate, delete, more…) instead of the fixed strip; a right panel with Properties | Structure
  (tree) | Layers tabs, resizable and collapsible; the library as a tall side panel (not a drawer
  over the model); a view cube and a minimal status bar; a **last operation** panel (like Blender's
  "Adjust Last Operation") to refine values after applying.
- Visual kit: tokens (colors, 12/11 px typography, 26 px rows, 16 px icons), dark and light themes,
  redesigned icons with a single stroke, visible focus, AA contrast, tooltips with shortcuts.
- Layout check at 1280×720, 1440×900, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 (cut-off text, overlap).
Files: `f3/ui/shell.ts` (rebuilt), `f3/ui/kit.ts`, `f3/ui/icons.ts`, `f3/ui/actions.ts`→`commands.ts`,
`f3/ui/palette.ts`.

### Phase 2 — Navigation, view and visual selection
- Camera: Shift+middle orbits (pivot selection/point under the cursor by depth), middle pans, the
  wheel zooms at the 3D point under the cursor; minimum distance ~0.05 m with an adaptive near
  plane; orthographic views (top, front, sides), view cube, frame selection/all respecting the
  panels, smooth transition; Revit (default)/SketchUp/Blender presets in the preferences.
- Screen-space selection and pre-selection outline (mask + depth), colors per mode.
- Screen-space handles, 8 px squares with a border, reused meshes (no allocation per frame).
Files: `f3/editor/view.ts`, new `f3/editor/camera.ts`, `f3/editor/handles.ts`, `f3/render/look.ts`.

### Phase 3 — Non-destructive core (user decision)
- `forma/4` schema: building = node graph (generators, operations, combination groups), automatic
  migration from `forma/3` (each solid becomes a generator; list order becomes a group).
- **Free solid**: an editable polyhedron (vertices/edges/faces with stable IDs) as a generator.
- Persistent references to sub-elements (generator side/vertex + Manifold face lineage); orphan
  nodes reported.
- Combine/uncombine, disable, reorder, isolate a node; incremental per-node evaluation with a cache
  (only rebuilds from the changed node), in the worker; during a drag only the shell.
- A "History/Structure" panel shows each element's stack (like Blender modifiers).
Files: `f3/model/schema.ts`, `f3/model/ops.ts`, `f3/model/modeling.ts`, `f3/eval/evaluate.ts`,
`f3/eval/body.ts`, `f3/kernel/kernel.ts`, `f3/io/migrate.ts`.

### Phase 4 — Selection and manipulation
- Modes: Object, Volume, Face, Edge, Vertex, Component (1–6 and on the bar; Shift combines);
  pre-highlight; Ctrl adds, Shift toggles (keeps), window/crossing box, Alt selects through.
- Select similar/linked/ring/loop; hide/isolate; **named selection sets** stored in the project.
- HUD around the object: move, rotate, scale, floor ±, enter, and the mode's actions.
- **Dimensions editable on the label itself**: clicking "18 m" opens a field, Enter applies, Tab
  goes to the next; works for sides, height, radius, offset, drag distance.
Files: `f3/editor/selection.ts`, new `f3/editor/tools/*`, `f3/editor/dims.ts`.

### Phase 5 — Modeling tools v2
- Tool base: live preview, value typed next to the cursor, options in the last operation panel,
  everything stored as a node.
- Offset (Clipper2, joins, clean collapse, on the plan/face/top, result as a node; "copy" becomes a
  group with thickness instead of an overlapping solid), Push/Pull, Extrude, Inset,
  Bevel/Chamfer/Fillet per edge, Split, Bridge, Revolve, Sweep (Follow Me), Loft, linear/grid/
  radial/path Array, Mirror, proportional Scale.
- **Align and Distribute** always visible on the HUD with multiple selection.
- **3D contact snapping**: on top, below, beside, inside, centered, on the faces of other volumes
  and components; boxes and faces as targets; merge = combination group (uncombinable).
- Building use with a real effect: floor presets, ground floor, facade rules and materials.
Files: `f3/model/modeling.ts`, `f3/editor/infer.ts`, new `f3/editor/tools/*`, dependency `clipper2-ts`.

### Phase 6 — Properties and Structure/Layers
- Inspector in short sections: Identity (name, type, layer, tags), Transform (X/Y/Z, rotation,
  W/D/H with a proportion lock), Floors (table: name, height, elevation, use), Use, Roof, Materials
  (project material slots), Facade, Structure, Lot/indices, Information (footprint area, built
  area, volume); fields with label dragging and expressions.
- Tree: icons per type, eye/lock always visible, search and filter, rename, drag to group; Layers
  in a tab of their own (color, active, visible, locked); categories as view filters.
Files: `f3/ui/inspector.ts`, `f3/ui/layers.ts`, `f3/model/layers.ts`.

### Phase 7 — Parametric catalog (hundreds of elements)
- Browser: a category tree with counts, a grid of real 3D thumbnails (rendered from the families
  and cached), search, tag filters, favorites, recents, drag, a preview with parameters on hover.
- New families and variations up to hundreds of types: windows and doors by operation/shape,
  stairs, railings, balconies, roofs and accessories, structure, **interior** (living room,
  kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, office furniture), **exterior** (vegetation, street furniture, pools,
  pergolas).
- **In-app family editor**: parameters with formulas (LenX, Copies, Copy), nested subcomponents,
  `PartSink` primitives, preview; save, import and export.
Files: `f3/families/*`, `f3/ui/catalog.ts`, `f3/render/thumbs.ts`, new `f3/ui/familyEditor.ts`.

### Phase 8 — Procedural materials and textures
- Project material library (by ID: editing one changes every use).
- Layered material editor: a PBR base (color, roughness, metal, normal, emission, opacity,
  scale/rotation/offset) + procedural generators (brick, stone, roof tile, wood, concrete, floor,
  metal, noise) + masks (dirt, wear, damp, edge) + decals; baked on the GPU (extends
  `render/procedural.ts`) with a cache; sphere/cube thumbnail; hundreds of presets.
- One place to apply: bucket + material slots in the inspector, the same library.
Files: `f3/render/procedural.ts`, `f3/render/finishes.ts`, `f3/ui/materials.ts`, new `f3/ui/materialEditor.ts`.

### Phase 9 — Interior, floors and walking
Floor section, interior walls, interior doors/windows, slabs with cuts, stairs between levels,
replicate floors, walk mode (request queue item 4 of TOOLS.md).

### Phase 10 — Performance, robustness and validation
- Split `editor.ts` by tool/mode; handles and outlines without allocation per frame; measure
  before/after (ms per frame, draw calls, triangles, rebuild ms) in every phase.
- The five validation buildings of PLAN.md made from scratch with the new interface only, plus a
  sequence that combines and uncombines and edits vertex/edge/face and goes back.
- Documentation: `docs/PLAN.md`, `INTERFACE.md`, `TOOLS.md`, `SELECTION.md` updated per phase.

## 5. Note on the browser state

To test, I copied the project saved in the browser and restored it at the end. On reload, defect A1
showed: the app refused the saved project and kept a copy in `forma3_project_invalid`. Both keys
(`forma3_project`, `forma3_project_invalid`) stayed in the 5792 storage; deleting them was blocked
by auto mode. The app opens the samples normally; Phase 0 fixes the cause.
